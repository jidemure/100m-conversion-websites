# $100M conversion-page QA checklist

## Strategy

- [ ] The audience, situation, desired outcome, offer, and primary conversion event are explicit.
- [ ] The headline matches the traffic promise and names a meaningful outcome.
- [ ] The mechanism or differentiator is understandable within the first screen or two.
- [ ] One primary CTA dominates; secondary actions do not compete.

## Psychology

- [ ] The framing emphasizes a true gain, outcome, identity, or useful perspective.
- [ ] One primary Life Force Eight desire is clear and appropriate to the audience.
- [ ] The page makes meaningful effort, expertise, materials, safeguards, or process visible.
- [ ] Images and language reflect the customer’s real context and vocabulary.
- [ ] Copy is easy to scan and each section has one clear job.
- [ ] The offer reduces uncertainty and makes the exchange easy to evaluate.

## Proof and claims

- [ ] Each material claim has nearby, relevant evidence or is labeled as a hypothesis.
- [ ] Testimonials identify enough context to be credible and are not over-edited into generic praise.
- [ ] Results include timeframe, baseline, audience, and conditions where relevant.
- [ ] No invented scarcity, fake urgency, dark patterns, or unsupported guarantees appear.
- [ ] Regulated or safety-related claims are reviewed and appropriately qualified.

## UX and implementation

- [ ] Mobile layout, load performance, contrast, type size, focus states, and keyboard use are acceptable.
- [ ] The CTA is visible at decision points and describes the next step.
- [ ] Forms ask only for information needed at that stage.
- [ ] Pricing, inclusions, exclusions, terms, refund/guarantee conditions, and required commitments are clear.
- [ ] Analytics distinguish primary conversion, micro-conversions, and guardrail outcomes.

## Testing

- [ ] The highest-impact unresolved assumption is written as a falsifiable hypothesis.
- [ ] The test has a defined control, variant, audience, metric, and decision rule.
- [ ] The team will avoid changing multiple high-impact variables without a reason.
